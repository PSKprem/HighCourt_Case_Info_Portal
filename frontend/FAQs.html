Frequently Asked Questions

How can I search for a case? +
You can search by either case number or petitioner name using the search bar on the home page.
Can I use partial names or case numbers? +
Yes, the system supports partial matches and will list close results.
What kind of cases are listed here? +
Only public cases from the High Court are shown. Confidential cases are excluded.
How often is the data updated? +
The data is updated every month or based on official court records release.
Can I download case details? +
Yes, available cases offer downloadable PDFs from the result section.
Is the website screen reader friendly? +
Yes, the website follows basic accessibility and semantic guidelines for assistive tech.
Who can I contact for wrong information? +
Reach out to the development/support team via the contact section or footer email.
