📊 Visualisation Summary

Our visual dashboard provides insightful graphs and trends derived from High Court case data. These visualisations help researchers, legal professionals, and the general public understand how the court system operates over time.

🔹 The Cases Per Year chart displays how many cases were judged each year. This helps identify which years saw a rise or drop in judicial activity.

🔹 The Cases Per Judge graph highlights which judges handled the most cases. It gives a sense of individual judge workload and experience.

🔹 The Cases Per Petitioner or Respondent chart reveals the most frequent participants in court cases. This could include governments, corporations, or individuals involved in multiple disputes.

🔹 The Most Active Months graph shows in which months the courts are busiest. This helps uncover seasonal trends in case judgments.

Each graph is generated dynamically based on real or dummy case data and can be viewed interactively through the "Visualisation" tab.
