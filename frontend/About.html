About This Project

This website serves as a powerful tool for government officials, lawyers, and citizens who need easy access to High Court case details. Whether you're looking for text information or downloadable PDFs, our system simplifies everything in one place.

🔍 Smart Case Search

Search by name or number and get instant results from selected High Courts.

📁 PDF & Text Access

Access full case documents in PDF or plain text formats based on your preference.

📊 Visual Statistics

View graphical reports and stats based on case status, state, and more.

🗣️ Sound-Based Search

For specially-abled users, voice search helps find case information easily.

🚀 Future Expansion

We aim to cover all Indian High Courts and add smart filtering features.
